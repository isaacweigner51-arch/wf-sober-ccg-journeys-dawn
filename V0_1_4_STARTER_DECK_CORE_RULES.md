# WF Sober CCG v0.1.4

- Four exact 40-card prebuilt decks.
- Copy limits: Bronze/Silver/Gold/Epic 3, Legendary 2, Platinum/Signature Gold 1.
- Maximum followers: 5.
- Maximum hand size: 9; overdrawn cards return to the bottom of the deck.
- Daily Progress is a persistent Purpose Recovery Skill that occupies a board slot and transforms into A Life Rebuilt at 6 Progress.
