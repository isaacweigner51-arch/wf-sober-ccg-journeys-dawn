# WF Sober CCG v0.4.1 — Drag Evolution Orbs

- Replaced the left-side text evolution menu with four circular star orbs.
- Each orb clearly shows its Play Point cost and star tier.
- Drag an available orb directly onto an unevolved friendly follower to evolve it.
- Invalid drops, Amulets, already-evolved followers, insufficient PP, and used orbs are rejected with clear messages.
- Used evolution orbs become dimmed checkmarked orbs.
- Evolution still allows the follower to attack enemy followers immediately, while attacking the enemy leader requires Breakthrough/Storm.
