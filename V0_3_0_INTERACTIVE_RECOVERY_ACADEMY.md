# v0.3.0 Interactive Recovery Academy

- Recovery Academy is permanently available from the main menu.
- Each class has a replayable scripted battle lesson.
- Objectives advance only when the player performs the required action in battle.
- Courage teaches Resolve through combat survival and follower destruction.
- Hope teaches Hope through healing, the Relapse Zone, and recovery.
- Serenity teaches Peace through Recovery Skills, healing, patience, and Sanctuary buffs.
- Purpose teaches Progress through Daily Progress, efficient PP use, milestones, and Walking Free.
- A persistent objective panel and tappable class-resource tracker guide the lesson.
- First-clear rewards remain one-time; completed lessons remain replayable.
