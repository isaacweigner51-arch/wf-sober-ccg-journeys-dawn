# v0.6.7 — Hand Control & Card Inspection

- Drag any card in the player's hand onto another card to rearrange hand order.
- Added an information button to every visible hand and battlefield card.
- Right-clicking a visible card also opens its details.
- Added a full card inspection overlay showing enlarged art, class, rarity, cost, stats, and complete effect text.
- Hand arrangement remains player-controlled and does not alter deck order or game rules.
