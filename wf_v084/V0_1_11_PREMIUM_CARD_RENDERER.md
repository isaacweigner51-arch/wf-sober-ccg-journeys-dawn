# v0.1.11 Premium Card Renderer

- Collection and crafting now use the same deterministic artwork pool as battle cards.
- Collection grid changed from eight cramped columns to six properly sized columns.
- Cards now fit inside a dedicated gold binder frame with consistent padding.
- Full card art occupies most of each card instead of a small strip.
- Card names, rarity, cost, stats, and effect have dedicated non-overlapping regions.
- Pack openings display five larger full-art cards with consistent spacing.
- Locked cards remain visible and dimmed so players can see what they are crafting.
