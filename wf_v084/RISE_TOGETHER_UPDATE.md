# Rise Together Update

- Replaced JD-029 Unyielding Challenger with **Rise Together**.
- Courage Legendary, 7 PP, 5/6, one copy per deck.
- Tracks Courage followers that enter play during the match.
- At 3 or more Courage followers entered, Rise Together evolves for free on Arrival.
- Its evolution permanently gives every Courage follower remaining in the deck +1/+1.
- While an evolved Rise Together remains in play, newly played Courage followers gain Determination for that turn.
- The deck-wide buff triggers only once per copy because an evolved card cannot evolve again.
