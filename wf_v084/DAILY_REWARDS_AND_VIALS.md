# RC3 Daily Rewards and Recovery Vials

## Five-day reward cycle
- Day 1: 1 card pack
- Day 2: 2 card packs
- Day 3: 3 card packs
- Day 4: 4 card packs
- Day 5: 4 card packs + 300 Recovery Vials

The cycle repeats after Day 5. Missing more than one calendar day resets the next claim to Day 1. Rewards can be claimed once per calendar day.

## Duplicate conversion values
- Bronze: 10 Vials
- Silver: 40 Vials
- Gold: 150 Vials
- Legendary: 600 Vials
- Signature Platinum: 1,500 Vials

A card converts only after the player already owns the maximum playable copies for its rarity.
