# v0.7.8 Audio Overhaul

- Replaced the short repetitive main-menu loop with a longer 84-second arrangement containing four evolving sections, a recurring melodic hook, changing percussion, and subtle wave transitions.
- Added a new 72-second battle theme with a stronger rhythmic pulse, layered arpeggios, recurring battle hook, and section changes.
- Battle music now starts immediately after battle audio initializes, rather than relying on a later gameplay callback.
- Increased battle music and SFX levels so battle is no longer silent.
- Increased class battlefield ambience volume.
- Kept separate card play, draw, attack, impact, heal, evolve, victory, and defeat effects active over the music.
