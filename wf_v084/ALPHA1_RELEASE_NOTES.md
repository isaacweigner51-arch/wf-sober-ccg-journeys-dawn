# WF Sober CCG — Alpha 1

## Added
- Four-card opening hand.
- **Second Chance** opening mechanic with selectable cards and visible Momentum cost.
- Replaced cards are temporarily removed, replacements are drawn, and the originals are shuffled back.
- **Momentum** resource with a visible battle control.
- The player going second begins with one Momentum charge.
- Second Chance grants the opponent 0/1/1/2 Momentum for replacing 1/2/3/4 cards.
- Momentum can be stored and spent for +1 temporary Play Point.
- AI spends Momentum when it allows an otherwise unaffordable card to be played.
- First player skips the first normal draw to reduce first-turn advantage.
- Recovery Academy expanded to seven interactive lessons.
- New interactive lesson: **Proving Yourself**, teaching Second Chance and Momentum by selecting cards and activating the resource.

## Mobile
- Landscape/mobile settings from RC3 retained.
- Touch card selection and large controls retained.

## Build
- App version: 0.9.0-alpha1
