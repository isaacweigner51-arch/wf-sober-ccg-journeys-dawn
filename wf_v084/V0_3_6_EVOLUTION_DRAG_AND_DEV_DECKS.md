# v0.3.6 — Evolution Combat, Direct Drag, and Developer Deck Curves

- Evolving grants immediate follower combat access. A follower evolved this turn may attack enemy followers, including protected targets only when normal Protector rules permit. It cannot attack the enemy leader that turn unless it has Breakthrough/Storm.
- Drag from a friendly follower directly onto an enemy follower or leader to attack. Touch release positions now use global coordinates.
- Cards that specifically say they cannot be attacked are respected.
- Developer Meta and Final Boss decks are owner-only.
- Owner developer decks can be used in standard AI battles; the AI always receives a legal prebuilt deck rather than an elite developer deck.
- Developer decks now use deliberate low/mid/high curves instead of selecting forty high-rarity finishers.
- Added balanced 4-cost Legendary options in each class.
