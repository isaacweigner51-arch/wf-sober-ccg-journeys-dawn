# v0.6.4 — The First Day

- Replaces the old demo-first flow with six guided training lessons.
- New players earn exactly 500 Gold once.
- Completion awards a selected legal 40-card starter deck.
- Sponsor is excluded from starter decks and remains a pack-only build-around card.
- Battle, Recovery Road, and Deck Builder are presented after training completion.
- Training can be replayed without duplicate rewards.
- New profiles begin with 0 Gold and 0 packs; existing saves are preserved.
