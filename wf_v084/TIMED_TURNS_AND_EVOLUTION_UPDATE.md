# v0.6.6 — Timed Turns & Evolution Update

- Leaders remain on the sides as a Walking Free visual identity and are slightly larger.
- Player turns are limited to 75 seconds.
- At 30 seconds remaining, a themed “Sitting on your steps?” slacking animation appears.
- At 10 seconds remaining, an urgent recovery-themed warning appears.
- The turn ends automatically at zero.
- Added a fourth Evolution option: 4 PP Transcend (+4/+4 and the class special ability).
- Enemy AI can also use the fourth Evolution option.
