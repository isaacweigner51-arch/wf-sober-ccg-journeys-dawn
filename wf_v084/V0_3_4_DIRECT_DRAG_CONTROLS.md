# v0.3.4 Direct Drag Controls

- Removed the battle card-details overlay entirely.
- Hand cards can be dragged directly to the follower or Recovery Skill field to play them.
- Friendly followers can be dragged directly onto an enemy follower or the enemy leader to attack.
- Dragging cancels normal click handling so no card menu appears.
- Recovery Skills no longer open a card-details popup during battle.
