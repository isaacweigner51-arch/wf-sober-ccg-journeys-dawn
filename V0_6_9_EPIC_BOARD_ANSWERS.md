# v0.6.9 — Epic Board Answers

Added three Epic spells without changing existing class amulets:

- Hope — Second Chance (7 PP): Transform the three highest-Attack enemy followers into 1/1 Newcomers and restore 3 defense.
- Purpose — Strategic Collapse (8 PP): Choose between dealing 5 to all enemy followers or returning all enemy followers with 5 or less Attack to hand; then draw a card.
- Serenity — Calm After the Storm (8 PP): Destroy all followers, restore 5 defense, and draw 2 if at least 6 followers were destroyed.

Courage keeps its proactive identity and receives no reset spell.
