# WF Sober CCG visual asset guide

This build uses procedural placeholder art so it works without external files. To make it commercially attractive, replace placeholders with a consistent art package.

## Highest-priority assets

1. **One leader portrait per class** — 1024×1024 PNG, transparent background preferred.
2. **Card art** — portrait orientation, ideally 1024×1536. Start with the 12 Platinum and Legendary cards before illustrating all 76.
3. **One battlefield background** — 1920×1080, with the center kept visually quiet for cards.
4. **Faction emblems** — square transparent PNG or SVG for Serenity, Courage, Purpose, Hope, and Universal.
5. **Card backs** — one general back plus optional class-specific backs.
6. **Audio** — card draw, summon, attack impact, damage, evolve, victory, and defeat.

## Art-direction decisions needed

- Fantasy, modern fantasy, science fantasy, or grounded recovery symbolism.
- Realistic, painterly, anime-inspired, comic, or stylized 3D.
- Whether human recovery language remains direct or is represented through fantasy metaphors.
- Preferred colors and logo treatment.

## Recommended first art batch

Create 8 polished pieces first: four leaders and four Platinum cards. This is enough to establish the visual identity before commissioning all 76 illustrations.
