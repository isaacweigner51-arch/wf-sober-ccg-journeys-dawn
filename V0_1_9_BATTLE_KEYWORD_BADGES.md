# WF Sober CCG v0.1.9 — Battle Keyword Badges

Followers now display visible battlefield badges for their important abilities instead of requiring players to reread card text.

Supported badges include:
- Protector / Ward
- Guidance / Bane
- Determination / Rush
- Breakthrough / Storm
- Restore / Drain
- Recovery
- Legacy / Last Words
- Arrival / Fanfare

Up to four badges are shown on each card to keep the battlefield readable. Hovering a badge on desktop shows its full keyword name.
