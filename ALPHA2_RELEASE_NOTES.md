# WF Sober CCG — Alpha 2 Brother Battle Core

## Working changes
- New landscape home screen designed as two large mobile panels instead of a compressed desktop column.
- Functional **VS Brother — Pass & Play** mode for one phone or tablet.
- Both players choose their class.
- Both players receive a private **Second Chance** opening-hand phase.
- The device displays a privacy/pass screen between every turn.
- The active player is always shown on the lower half of the battlefield, so their hand remains usable and the other player's hand remains hidden.
- Existing turn timer, Momentum, evolution, card inspection, hand reordering, Relapse Zone, and 40-card decks remain active.
- Deck-selection leader artwork now uses aspect-centered display rather than cropping faces and emblems.

## Important
This build provides immediate two-player play on one Android phone or tablet. Separate-device online room-code play still requires an internet lobby/relay service and is not represented as completed in this build.
