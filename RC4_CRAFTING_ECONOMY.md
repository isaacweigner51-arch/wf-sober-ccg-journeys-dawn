# WF Sober CCG — RC4 Crafting Economy

## Repeating daily track
The reward sequence repeats every five claims. After Day 5, the next claim is Day 1. Missing a calendar day does not reset the track.

- Day 1: 1 pack
- Day 2: 2 packs
- Day 3: 3 packs
- Day 4: 4 packs
- Day 5: 4 packs + 300 Recovery Vials

## Duplicate and vial values
- Bronze: 10 Vials
- Silver: 40 Vials
- Gold: 150 Vials
- Legendary: 600 Vials
- Signature Platinum duplicate: 1,500 Vials

Bronze and Silver cards can be manually vialed from Collection & Crafting. Copies used in the currently saved deck are protected. Gold, Legendary, and Signature Platinum cards convert only when a pack pull exceeds the ownership limit.

## Creation costs
- Bronze: 50 Vials
- Silver: 150 Vials
- Gold: 500 Vials
- Legendary: 2,000 Vials
- Signature Platinum: pack-only and cannot be created
