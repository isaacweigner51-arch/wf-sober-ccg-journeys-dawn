# v0.1.6 Card Art & Crafting Layout Fix

- Crafting cards now always show their full card artwork, including unowned cards.
- Unowned cards are dimmed with a LOCKED badge instead of being replaced by a question mark.
- Compact crafting cards use a dedicated layout so rarity no longer overlaps the card name.
- Second Chance now displays full card art, name, rarity, cost, stats, and shortened effect text.
- Card art uses aspect-fit centering so it is not cropped.
