# WF Sober CCG v0.1.3 — Sponsor Paths & Story Chapter 1

## The Sponsor
- Remains the Neutral Signature Platinum.
- Creates a Sponsee on Arrival and one more at the end of each controller turn while board space remains.
- Each Sponsee inherits the leader's class path:
  - Courage: 3/2 with Determination.
  - Hope: 2/3 and restores 3 Defense through Legacy.
  - Serenity: 2/3 Protector.
  - Purpose: gains one Progress when it enters play.
- All Sponsees receive one Sponsor protection and restore at least 1 Defense when they enter the Relapse Zone.

## Story Mode — Chapter 1
- Five battles with sequential unlocking.
- Stage rewards: packs and Gold.
- Completion is saved locally.
- Chapter 1 rewards total 4 packs and 700 Gold.
