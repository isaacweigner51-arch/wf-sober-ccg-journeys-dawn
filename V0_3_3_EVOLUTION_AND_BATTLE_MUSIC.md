# WF Sober CCG v0.3.3 — Evolution & Battle Music

- Evolution Paths are fully playable in battle: Ascend, Awaken, and Transcend.
- Select an available path, then select an unevolved friendly follower.
- Evolution spends PP, grants the listed stat increase, enables immediate attacks, and triggers evolved abilities.
- Used evolution paths remain unavailable for the rest of the match.
- Added a dedicated evolution sound and retained the full-screen evolution animation.
- Battle music now loops continuously and changes as the match reaches mid, late, and critical stages.
- Raised battle music volume and automatically restarts it if playback stops.
- Evolution buttons now clearly disable when the player lacks PP, has no follower, or it is not their turn.
