# Journey's Dawn Reforged — Card Balance Pass

This build replaces the original high-cost stat-heavy starter decks with cohesive class engines and a playable mana curve.

## Core rules
- 40-card decks
- 10-card hand limit
- Overdrawn cards are Revived to the bottom of the deck
- Destroyed followers enter the Relapse Zone
- Bronze/Silver: 3 copies; Gold: 2; Legendary/Signature Platinum: 1

## Class identities
- Hope: Renew, healing, protection, Recovery, and rebuilding from the Relapse Zone
- Courage: Charge, Rush, direct pressure, self-risk, and board-wide rallies
- Serenity: Calm, exhaustion, return-to-hand control, removal, and board resets
- Purpose: Progress, maximum Play Point growth, card draw, cost reduction, and earned finishers
- Neutral: Sponsor/Sponsee, Recovery, Fellowship, Rock Bottom, and Second Chance

## Major gameplay additions
- Relapse Zone tracking for both players
- Revival to the battlefield or hand
- Board-clearing effects
- Targeted follower damage
- Exhaust/freeze effects
- Return-to-hand effects
- Hand cost reduction
- Board-wide buffs
- Class-defining Signature Platinum effects

## Starter-deck curve
The starter decks now use low-, middle-, and high-cost cards rather than being dominated by expensive followers. Each deck includes setup, interaction, draw, recovery, and finishers.
