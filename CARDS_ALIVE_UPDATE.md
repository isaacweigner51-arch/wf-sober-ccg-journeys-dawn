# Cards Alive Update

This build adds motion and visual feedback directly to the card system:

- Subtle living artwork drift on every visible card
- Mouse-position parallax while hovering
- Hand-card lift and tilt
- Board-card lift and scale on hover
- Animated rarity aura around frames
- Legendary, Platinum, and evolved shine sweep
- Pulsing ready-to-attack cards
- Existing summon, damage, attack, and death animations retained

The project remains Journey's Dawn content-wise, but the build folder and archive are renamed to **WF Sober CCG - Living Cards Alpha**.
