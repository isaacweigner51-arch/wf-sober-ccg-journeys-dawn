# v0.6.3 — Sponsor Deck Fix

- The Sponsor is no longer automatically included in Walking Free/Purpose or any other prebuilt deck.
- Starter collections no longer grant The Sponsor automatically; it remains a Signature Platinum pack pull.
- The Sponsor remains fully available in Deck Builder once owned, allowing players to deliberately build a Sponsor/Sponsee deck around it.
- Existing saves containing the old automatic Sponsor copy are migrated: Sponsor is removed and the open slot is filled with a legal owned card.
- All prebuilt battle decks remain exactly 40 cards.
